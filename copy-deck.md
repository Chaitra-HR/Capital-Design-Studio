# Capital Design Studio: homepage copy deck (draft 1)

**Goal:** speak directly to the needs, desires and problems of financial advisors and wealth management firms. The tone is calm, confident and plain-spoken, never salesy. Design and layout stay exactly as they are. Every line below fits an existing slot, at roughly the same length.

**Flow (borrowed from Beetle Beetle, mapped onto our sections):**

| Beetle Beetle move | Our section | What it does for an advisor |
|---|---|---|
| Painful truth in the headline | Hero | Names the outcome they want (the right clients) |
| "You're asking yourself…" | Quick check | Lets them recognise their own symptoms |
| "Here's the bad news" | Problem | Why the usual fixes don't work |
| "Here's the good news" + what we do | Solution | One root cause, one fix, three parts |
| Proof | Client stories | People like them, in their words |
| How we work | Process | Removes the fear of a long, messy project |
| Agencies vs us | Comparison | Why a specialist, not a generalist |
| "Your time is priceless" / fit | Founder + FAQ | Who they'll work with, and who it's for |
| Free audit CTA | Final CTA | Low-risk next step |

**Advisor truths the copy leans on:**
- Most prospects look you up before the first call, even after a warm referral.
- Advisor websites tend to use the same words (fiduciary, holistic, personalised), so prospects compare on fees instead of fit.
- Wrong-fit enquiries, below your minimum or the wrong kind of client, waste time.
- Multi-advisor firms often describe themselves differently, advisor to advisor.
- Compliance review slows everything, and generic agencies don't plan for it.
- Advisors are busy, so they don't want to write their own copy.

Lines marked ⚠️ need a fact from you before launch.

### Tone guardrails used
- No performance promises (no "more AUM", "guaranteed leads" or "% growth").
- No pressure tactics or scarcity claims we can't stand behind.
- The prospect is "clients", never "leads" or "customers".
- Plain words over jargon: "why you", "the right clients", "compliance review".
